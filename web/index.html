§ 01 — The gap

The HIMS weekend

On the weekend of 29–31 August 2026 a memecoin routed its main liquidity against tokenized Hims & Hers on Robinhood Chain. Every dollar of meme demand became a forced bid for the stock wrapper — while the exchange was shut, the issuer couldn't mint, and there was no live price to arbitrage against. Across the chain's top pools, none used a dynamic fee. Nothing priced the closed market.

§ 03 — How it works

Cheap to restore the peg. Expensive to stretch it.

Deployed parameters, from script/Deploy.s.sol. All immutable.

MarketWhere the swap endsBuyer paysSplit
Open Anywhere. The last-close anchor follows prices that hold during market hours. 0.30% All to LPs. Hook: nothing.
Closed Closer to the anchor than it started, without crossing it 0.50% LPs 0.47% · hook 0.03% (15% of the 0.20% surcharge)
Closed Further from the anchor — or across it, so an overshoot pays too 0.50% + driftmin(|end tick − anchor| × 0.002%, 4.50%) · max 5.00% Drift donated to in-range LPs, minus the hook's 15%

Drift is charged on where the swap ends. It's measured on the real post-swap price, so pushing price far in one big swap pays the full distance. A swap back toward the anchor that overshoots it pays drift on the overshoot.

The anchor is time-weighted. Prices that hold during market hours reach full weight after 30 minutes, so a push just before the close barely moves it. Closed-market prices carry no weight.

The hook is paid out of the surcharges, never on top. While closed the LP fee is 0.47% and the hook takes 0.03% through its return delta, so buyers pay 0.50% either way.

Base 0.30% · closed surcharge +0.20% · drift 0.002% per tick (≈1 bp of price) of final distance, capped at +4.50% · contract hard cap on base + surcharges 10% · hook's share 15% of the surcharges (hard cap 20%), split 30% protocol / 70% pool operator, paid out pull-based. The live panel's quoteFee is the fee for a marginal swap; a larger swap pays drift on its end position. Session 09:30–16:00 ET with US daylight saving; NYSE holidays and 13:00 early closes are computed by rule, so the calendar never expires.

§ 05 — Safety

Nobody can turn the dial.

  • No ownerNo admin, no pause, no proxy. Nothing to upgrade, nothing to seize.
  • ImmutableEvery fee parameter, the unscheduled-closure list and both fee recipients are fixed at construction.
  • ≤ 10%MAX_TOTAL_FEE caps base + surcharges, checked in the constructor.
  • ≤ 20%MAX_SKIM_BIPS caps the hook's share — taken out of the surcharges, never on top and never from the base fee.
  • onlyPoolManagerEvery hook callback rejects any caller but the v4 PoolManager.
  • Dynamic feeafterInitialize reverts unless the pool is a dynamic-fee pool.
  • No LP accessNo liquidity callbacks. The hook can't touch positions; drift is donated to in-range LPs.
  • No oracleThe session clock runs from block.timestamp: DST, NYSE holidays and early closes by rule. No keeper, no list to expire.
